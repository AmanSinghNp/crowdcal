No amount of fine-tuning caught Jev.

When a model says 0.7, do about 70% of people agree? We compared each model's probability of entailment with the share of 100 human annotators who chose entailment, on 3,113 deliberately ambiguous ChaosNLI items. The score is soft Brier, (p − q)²: lower means the model's confidence tracks the crowd.

  • 3,113 test items
  • 100 annotators each
  • 5 models
  • 4 training sizes
  • Pre-registration
  • Code and raw data
